ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Meet Zandro Justine

My name is Zandro Justine F. Labasano, a Biologist, proud graduate of UPMin Batch 2025, and now 26 years old with a professional background in game development. Transitioning into the medical field, my foundation in biological sciences and interactive systems has strengthened my analytical problem-solving, attention to detail, and dedication to quality patient care.

This e-portfolio presents my Physical Diagnosis laboratory output, demonstrating my ability to integrate theoretical knowledge with practical clinical assessment skills. It reflects my growth, competencies, and commitment to advancing my expertise in the medical field.
