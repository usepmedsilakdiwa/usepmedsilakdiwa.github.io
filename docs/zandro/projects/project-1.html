Headache in a 28-Year-Old Female

This written report presents a comprehensive health history and focused physical diagnosis case involving a 28-year-old female with a progressively worsening headache of two days’ duration. The report describes the patient’s symptoms, relevant medical and family history, personal and social history, and review of systems.

Presenting concern Progressively worsening headache, rated 8/10
Reported associated symptoms Nausea, photophobia, and phonophobia
Relevant history Maternal history of migraine
Clinical impression in the report Suggestive of migraine without aura; further assessment is indicated
