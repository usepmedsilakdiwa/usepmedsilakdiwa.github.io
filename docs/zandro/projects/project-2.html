FEATURED CASE · PDX GROUP 3

COPD Screening via Spirometry in a 60-Year-Old Male

This clinical case analysis evaluates the diagnostic accuracy of routine spirometry screening for chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) in a 60-year-old male with a history of smoking. Using a population model of 1,000 high-risk patients at a 20% prevalence rate, the report breaks down true and false diagnostic outcomes.

Patient profile & risk 60-year-old asymptomatic male with a smoking history (20% group prevalence)
Diagnostic test Routine spirometry screening for airflow limitation
Test accuracy metrics 80% Sensitivity (160 TP, 40 FN) · 85% Specificity (680 TN, 120 FP)
Clinical conclusion Effective initial screening tool, though findings require clinical correlation
