Systematic Measurement of Vital Signs and Anthropometric BMI

This video demonstration presents a paired clinical role-play of core vital sign assessments and body mass index (BMI) computation. It covers pre-procedure preparation and consent, palpatory and auscultatory blood pressure measurement, one-minute heart and respiratory rate evaluation, temperature measurement, and height-and-weight BMI determination with patient explanation and counseling.

Patient preparation & consent Greeting, introduction, identifying data, procedure explanation, consent, and pre-test rest checks for exertion, smoking, or caffeine
Blood pressure assessment Palpatory estimation (+30 mmHg maximum inflation), cuff placement above the antecubital crease, slow deflation, and systolic/diastolic interpretation
Cardiopulmonary & temperature One-minute radial pulse and respiratory rate/rhythm/depth/effort checks alongside tympanic or axillary temperature measurement
BMI & patient communication Height and weight measurement, BMI calculation, clear explanation of findings, patient questions, and closing etiquette
